# Render plan

Scene: `blender/food_forest.blend` (generated by `blender/build_food_forest.py`, 450 frames, 30 fps, 1920x1080).
Cycles on OptiX (RTX 3060), 64 samples with adaptive threshold 0.02, OptiX denoiser, motion blur, bloom, grain and
vignette in the compositor.

Measured at 1920x1080 (frames 20, 100, 170, 230, 330, 410): 32.0, 15.1, 42.4, 18.6, 13.3, 31.3 s, mean 25 s per frame.
Shot 1 and shot 2 are heaviest (close, many alpha leaves); shot 5 and 6 are lighter.

Estimate: 450 frames x 25 s = 3.1 h, plus about 30 s of scene load for each of the 6 chunks, so about 3.3 h, well under
the 12 h limit. No quality was cut. The frames render at below-normal priority, so they slow down when An uses the GPU.

Commands (PowerShell, from this folder):
- `.\render.ps1` starts or resumes in the background and prints the worker pid.
- `.\render.ps1 -Status` shows frames done and the log tail.
- `.\render.ps1 -Stop` stops the worker and its Blender.
Finished frames (`render\frame_####.png` above 50 KB) are skipped, so a rerun resumes.

After a timing change, rebuild with `blender -b -P blender\build_food_forest.py`; this also rewrites `events.json`.
