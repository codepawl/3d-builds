# Fruit fly adult life: flight, courtship, mating, egg laying

Beat 5 of the film (after eclosion, before the opening's egg laying). Scale for close-ups stays 1 mm = 240 px (the
opening fly); wide shots use 1 mm = 48 px (fly scale 0.2). Numbers: M = measured in a primary source, I = inferred,
G = guessed. Sources are from the literature named; footage check still to do for courtship (watch-video at 8 fps).

## Flight

- Wing beat about 200 to 230 Hz (M, Dickinson lab high-speed work). At 30 fps every frame catches a different phase,
  so on film the wings read as a blurred fan between the top and bottom of the stroke, never as a sharp wing.
- Voluntary takeoff: the wings rise fully first, then the middle legs extend and push off; an escape jump is the
  reverse, legs first (M, Card and Dickinson 2008). Our takeoff is voluntary: wings up on one beat, jump on the next.
- Free flight is straight segments joined by body saccades: a turn of about 90° done in about 50 ms (M, Tammero and
  Dickinson 2002; Muijres et al. 2014). A saccade sits on each beat; straight flight between them.
- Flies gather on fermenting fruit, where courtship and egg laying happen (M). So the flight goes from the whole
  orange of the opening to a cut half where a female feeds.

## The compound eye (the hexagon beat)

- About 750 to 800 ommatidia per eye (M), packed as hexagons; each sees one spot, so the fly's view is a mosaic of
  about 800 pixels. The female seen "through his eye" is a hexagon mosaic of her colours.

## Courtship (Bastock and Manning 1955 sequence, M)

1. Orienting: the male turns to face her.
2. Following: he walks after her as she moves, keeping about one body length away.
3. Tapping: he touches her abdomen with a front leg (tastes her pheromones).
4. Singing: he extends one wing about 90° out to the side and vibrates it. Pulse song: pulses about 35 ms apart (M,
   interpulse interval of D. melanogaster); sine song near 150 Hz (M). The wing shows as a small blur at the tip.
5. Licking, then attempting copulation.
- Male vs female (M): male smaller, abdomen tip rounded and black (fused dark bands), sex combs on the front legs;
  female larger, abdomen pointed with separate stripes.

## Mating and egg laying

- Copulation lasts about 20 minutes (M); the male mounts from behind on her back, front legs on her abdomen. In the
  film: one beat, gentle, no detail.
- Egg laying (M): the female taps the fruit with her proboscis, extrudes the ovipositor and pushes an egg into soft
  flesh, the two dorsal appendages left sticking out to breathe. She can lay about 50 a day (I from lab counts).
  This is the opening frame: the film loops there.
