# Fruit fly: late embryo, hatching and first instar larva (beat 3, about 6 s)

Written before drawing. Sources: time-lapse videos on YouTube, watched frame by frame with `watch-video`:
"Drosophila embryogenesis: from fertilization to larva hatching" (tglab), "Fruit Fly: From Embryo to Larva" (Live
Science, EMBL footage), "Drosophila Embryo Moves Before Hatching" (Ellie Heckscher's lab); Bing Images results for
first instar larva, mouth hooks and the larval tracheal system. Measured = in a source; inferred = general knowledge
or read off the footage.

Scale: 1 µm = 1.52 px, as in beat 2 (egg 500 µm = 760 px).

## Parts

| Part | Built from | Size | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | head (pointed), 3 thoracic and 8 abdominal segments, blunt tail | at hatching about as long as the egg, 0.5 to 0.6 mm (inferred) | white and translucent; the gut shows through |
| Segment boundaries | one groove between each pair of segments | 12 grooves | thin lines across the body |
| Denticle belts | rows of tiny hooks on the belly side of each segment, used to grip when crawling | a band at the front of each segment | short dark ticks (Wikipedia: ventral denticle bands) |
| Mouth hooks | a pair of black hooks inside the pointed head | small | the darkest thing on the larva; they rake forward when it eats or cuts the shell |
| Tracheae | two main air tubes along the back, from the front spiracles to the rear spiracles | full length | silvery once they fill with air shortly before hatching (tglab footage: dark lines appear along the top) |
| Rear spiracles | two short stubs at the tail | small | dark tips |
| Gut | a coiled tube made from the yolk mass, which shrinks as it folds (tglab) | most of the inside | dark, folded loops |

## How it develops and hatches

1. The banded embryo becomes segments: grooves form where the engrailed stripes were (one stripe per segment's rear
   edge), so the stripes of beat 2 can turn into the grooves.
2. The dark yolk mass shrinks and folds into gut loops (tglab, about 12 to 24 h).
3. The tracheae fill with air and show as two lines along the back; the mouth hooks darken.
4. The larva moves inside the shell: peristaltic waves (segments bunch up and stretch out in a wave along the body)
   and the head swings side to side (Heckscher footage).
5. It cuts out at the front with its mouth hooks and crawls out head first, leaving an empty, crumpled shell (tglab).
6. Crawling: a wave of contraction runs from tail to head; the denticle belts grip as each segment lifts and lands.
   It starts eating right away (yeast and fruit).

## Beat plan

0.0 to 2.0 s blueprint (continues beat 2): stripes turn into grooves, gut folds, tracheae fill, mouth hooks show,
first peristaltic waves. 2.0 s cut to the paper illustration: the egg on the fruit flesh, larva visible through the
translucent shell. 2.2 to 3.8 s it wriggles and the front of the shell splits. 3.8 to 5.2 s it crawls out head first.
5.2 to 6.0 s the empty crumpled shell stays behind and the larva heads for a cluster of yeast cells.
