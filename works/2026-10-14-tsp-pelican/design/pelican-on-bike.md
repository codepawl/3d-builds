# Pelican on a bicycle: parts and sizes

Scale: film pixels, wheel tyre radius 146 px. A 26-inch wheel has a tyre radius of about 0.33 m,
so 1 m is about 440 px (inferred). No photo research was done for this benchmark run: proportions
come from general knowledge of the brown/great white pelican and a cruiser bicycle (guessed, not measured).

Motion phases (ride): spin-up over 1.5 s, then 20 rpm cadence; wheels turn 40/16 = 2.5 x the crank;
each foot stays flat on its pedal, the knee leads forward on the down-stroke.
