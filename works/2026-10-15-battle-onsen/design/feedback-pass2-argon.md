# Owner notes on your first pass

- The camera path wanders off the subject: halfway it looks at tree trunks and empty snow, and at the end a large object sits right against the lens.
- The pines are stacked cone shapes and the rocks and snow are smooth toy-like blobs; this is the low-poly look to remove.
- The colours are saturated and cartoon-like (pink snow, orange glow); aim for a real winter photograph.
