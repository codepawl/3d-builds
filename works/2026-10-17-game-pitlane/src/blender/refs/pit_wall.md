# pit_wall - pit wall and team strategy stand

Real type: concrete pit wall with a debris fence between pit lane and track; the team stand on the pit-lane side is a
raised desk with stools, monitor banks, roof canopy and steps.

## Dimensions
| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Wall | 1.1 m + 2.5 m fence | pit_wall/01, garage/03 |
| Stand length | 4.5 m | pit_wall/01 (four seats) |
| Desk height | 1.1 m (bar stools) | pit_wall/01 against a 1.8 m person |
| Roof height | 2.7 m | pit_wall/01 |

## Parts
Wall blocks, fence posts and mesh, base with steps, desk, four stools, monitor banks (screen glow), roof, antenna,
blank team plate.
