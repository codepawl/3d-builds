Proportions: long bonnet (front axle to A-pillar about 1.3 m), cabin set back, fastback.

## Parts
Bonnet with vents, grille, headlights, glazing, side vents, diffuser, spoiler, four wheels (separate), roof light bar
(amber and green glow), mirrors. Variant: medical car (estate shape, same rules).
