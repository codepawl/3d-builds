# race_car - open-wheel single-seater (current F1-class formula car, 2026 rules envelope)

Real type: carbon monocoque single-seater, open cockpit with halo, front and rear wings, sidepods, airbox over the
driver, engine and gearbox behind the cockpit, four exposed wheels on wishbone suspension. Our design is an original
body and livery for the made-up "Halvard" team (car numbers 7 and 22) plus three rival liveries; no real team shapes,
logos or colours copied.

## Proportions
- Length : width 2.85 : 1, length : height 5.7 : 1.
- Wheel diameter : length 0.13. Nose tip 1.05 ahead of the front axle and low (0.15-0.30).
- Cockpit opening about 0.9 behind the front axle; airbox top just behind the driver helmet.
- Sidepods start about 1.0 behind the front axle, full width about 1.5, taper to a narrow coke-bottle rear.

## Parts (front to back)
Front wing (3 elements, endplates) - nose cone - front wishbones and push rod - front wheels (moving: spin, steer) -
monocoque - mirrors on stalks - halo - cockpit, steering wheel, helmet - sidepods with inlets - airbox and roll hoop
with camera pod - engine cover with fin - floor, plank, edge wings - rear wishbones - rear wheels (moving: spin) -
diffuser - rear wing with DRS flap (moving: opens) and beam wing - rain light (red glow) - number plates.
