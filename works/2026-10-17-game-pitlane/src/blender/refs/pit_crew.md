# pit_crew - pit stop crew member, garage mechanic, marshal

Real type: team mechanic in fire-resistant overalls, helmet with visor, gloves and boots for the stop crew; garage
mechanics in team shirts with headsets; marshals in orange overalls. Stop roles (pit_crew/01, /02 top-down): front
jack, rear jack, four wheel-gun operators, four tyre-off and four tyre-on carriers, two side stabilisers; release is
signalled by the light gantry.
