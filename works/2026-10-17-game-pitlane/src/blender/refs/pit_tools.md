# pit_tools - front jack, rear jack, wheel gun, car stands, release light gantry

Real type: quick-lift front jack (long pole, saddle under the nose, two wheels, swivel pedal), shorter rear jack,
pneumatic wheel gun (single centre nut, socket, hose), garage car stands, overhead release light gantry.

## Dimensions
| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Front jack length | 1.8 m, wheels 0.20 m | pit_crew/01 (jack about 2.5 x tyre diameter 0.71) |
| Rear jack length | 1.1 m | pit_crew/02 against rear wing span 0.95 |
| Lift | about 0.12 m wheel clearance | pit_crew/03 |
| Wheel gun | body 0.36 long, 0.12 dia, socket 0.07, hose 25 mm | pit_tools/02 (1/2 in wrench about 0.22 m; pit guns about 1.5x) |
| Release gantry | 2.6 m high, 2.4 m span, lamp heads 0.22 m | pit_crew/03 |
| Car stand | 0.45 m cradle | garage/01 |

## Parts
Front jack: pole, T handle, saddle, pedal, two wheels on a frame. Rear jack: same, shorter. Wheel gun: grip, trigger,
body, socket, hose. Gantry: two posts, beam, lamp heads (red, green, amber glow), cable tray.
