# Castle cutaway (photoreal): measurements

Original design, Blender units are metres, Z up. The castle follows real 14th-century English and French types.
Reference values are the published figures as I recall them (not re-fetched online in this run), so each is marked
"approx". Our numbers are chosen inside the real ranges.

## Our measurements

- Ground z=0; moat water z=-4.5 (a 4.5 m drop, to show the dungeon at the cut); moat 11 m wide (Bodiam is wider, we
  need the bridge readable); banks slope 5 m.
- Curtain wall: rectangle x -40..40, y -27..35 (80 x 62 m), 2.8 m thick, 9 m above ground (13.5 m above the water),
  wall-walk with 1.4 m merlon parapet outside and 0.9 m low wall inside, arrow slits every 6 m, 0.6 m batter.
- Corner towers: diameter 10 m, 14 m above ground, machicolated corbel ring at 12.5 m, merlon parapet, two with
  conical slate roofs (height 11 m).
- Gatehouse: twin drum towers diameter 8 m (centres x -30 and -18, y -27), 16 m high, passage 4.2 m wide, 5.5 m
  high, portcullis in the arch, drawbridge leaf 4 x 7 m hinged at the sill, two chains to windows at z=7.5, stone
  pier at 7 m, hoarding on the top.
- Keep (outer): x 4..24, y -25..-7 (20 x 18 m), 29.5 m to the parapet, plus slate roofed back turrets (diameter 8,
  to 34 m) and a stair turret. Walls: 3.4 m (z<9), 3.0 m (9..22), 2.5 m (above). The front (-Y) face is cut open.
- Levels (z): dungeon -4.5..0 (vaulted, two cells), stores 0..4.5, kitchen 4.5..9, chapel 9..14.5 (barrel vault),
  great hall 14.5..22 (7.5 m), lord's chamber 22..27.5, roof deck 27.5..29.5. Floors: oak, 0.5 m thick, joists
  0.3 x 0.4 m at 0.9 m.
- Bailey: cobble and packed earth, well (diameter 2.4 m), two lean-to sheds with slate and thatch, wall stairs.
- Camera: 35 mm full frame, f/4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 600 frames.
- Human scale check: a 1.8 m person beside the gate passage fills 33% of its height.
