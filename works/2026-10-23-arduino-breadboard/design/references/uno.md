# Reference sheet (from the Arduino docs, no images shipped)
Uno R3: 68.6 x 53.4 mm, PCB 1.6 mm. USB-B (about 16 x 12 x 11 mm) upper left, barrel jack (14 x 9 x 11 mm) lower left, ATmega328P DIP-28
(34.8 x 7.5 x 3.5 mm) lower right in a socket, ATmega16U2 near the USB, 16 MHz crystal, reset button top left, ICSP header right,
four status LEDs (L, TX, RX, ON). Headers: top 10 + 8 pins (SCL SDA AREF GND 13 12 ~11 ~10 ~9 8 | gap | 7 ~6 ~5 4 ~3 2 TX1 RX0),
bottom 8 + 6 pins (NC IOREF RESET 3V3 5V GND GND VIN | A0-A5). Header height 8.5 mm.
Breadboard: half size, see FACTS.md. Parts: 5 mm LED, 1/4 W axial resistor 6.3 x 2.3 mm, 6 mm tactile switch, 10 k rotary pot, SG90.
