# 1950s Wisconsin farmstead, exterior only (farmhouse, summer kitchen shed, barn, outbuildings)

## Real type
A central Wisconsin sand-country farmstead, made of:
- an upright-and-wing frame farmhouse: a two-storey gable-front upright with a one-and-a-half-storey side wing and a
  porch;
- a detached one-storey summer kitchen shed;
- a gambrel barn on a fieldstone base;
- a lean-to machine shed, a woodshed, a privy and a hand pump.

No interior is modelled (fact file, section 5). The house burned on 20 Mar 1958 and no open source documents its plan,
so this is a generic period farmstead.

## Proportions
The upright roof pitch is about 10/12, the wing ridge sits below the upright's eave, and the barn's length to width is
1.34.

## Parts list
- House: stone foundation, gable-front upright, wing, front porch with two posts, chimney. The windows are dark, since
  the farm had no electricity on the night of 16 Nov 1957.
- Summer kitchen: door (a separate node), stovepipe.
- Barn: big door, hay door, cupola.
