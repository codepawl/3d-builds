# Generic 1950s half-ton pickup (the Worden store truck)

## Real type
An American half-ton pickup of 1953-56, modelled to the size class of the Ford F-100, the most common truck of the
period. The store truck's make is not known (fact file, section 6), so it is labelled generic and carries no badges,
grille lettering or plates.

## Proportions
The cab is about 1.3 m long and the bed about 1.9 m (6.5 ft). A narrow bed has separate rear fenders outside it and a
running board between the front and rear fenders. The hood top is at about 1.15 m and the roof at about 1.9 m.

## Parts list
- Front: wide grille with bumper, round headlamps in the fender fronts.
- Side: front fender over the wheel, door, running board, rear fender, bed side.
- Back: tailgate, tail lamps, bumper.
- Moving parts: two doors, the tailgate (hinged at the bed floor) and four wheels.
