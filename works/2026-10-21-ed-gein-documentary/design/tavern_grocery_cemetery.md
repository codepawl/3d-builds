# Small rural tavern (generic), small country grocery (generic), cemetery props

## Real type
- Tavern: a one-and-a-half-storey frame crossroads tavern with a gable front, side windows and a rear shed, as found
  in rural Portage County. The real lot in the Town of Pine Grove is unknown (fact file, section 1), so the map shows
  an area marker.
- Grocery: a one-storey frame false-front country store with a porch roof on posts, like the Hill grocery in West
  Plainfield (lot unknown).
- Cemetery: Wisconsin small-town cemetery furniture of 1880-1950: an iron picket fence, a double gate between stone
  piers with ball caps, upright slabs, a small obelisk, flat markers and a low family stone. No inscriptions, no names
  and no specific graves (fact file, section 5).

No signs or brand panels on any of them.
