# Generic 1950s police car

## Real type
An American full-size four-door sedan of the mid-1950s, as used by county sheriffs and city police, modelled to the
size of the 1955 Ford. The doors are a light shade (a two-tone police scheme) and there is one roof lamp, emissive
blue in the app's palette. No lettering, seal, number or livery.

## Proportions
A flat belt line at about 0.95 m along the whole car, a wraparound windshield, a greenhouse about 45% of the length,
and hooded headlamps.

## Parts list
- Front: bumper, wide grille, headlamps.
- Side: four doors, wheel openings, B pillar.
- Roof: one lamp.
- Back: trunk, tail lamps, bumper.
- Moving parts: four doors, four wheels and the roof lamp (`police_car_roof_lamp`, pivot at its base).
