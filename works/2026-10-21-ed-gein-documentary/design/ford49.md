# 1949 Ford two-door sedan (Tudor)

## Real type
The 1949 Ford, the first post-war Ford body: slab sides with integrated fenders, a notchback, two doors and a spinner
grille. The fact file records Gein's car as a maroon 1949 Ford sedan without a body style. The two-door is modelled
because both CC photos show Tudors. No badges or plates.

## Proportions
Length to width 2.75 and length to height 3.2. The hood line and the belt line sit at one height, about 1.0 m. The
greenhouse is about 40% of the length, set behind centre, and the rear overhang is longer than the front.

## Parts list
- Front: chrome bumper with two guards, a horizontal grille bar with a centre spinner, two round headlamps in the
  fender fronts.
- Side: slab side, wheel openings (the rear partly covered), a long door with a chrome strip, a thick C pillar, a
  small rear quarter window.
- Back: notch trunk, tail lamps, bumper.
- Moving parts: two doors (hinged at the A pillar) and four wheels.
