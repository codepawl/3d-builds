# Plainfield Main Street around the North St junction (with the hardware store and the bank)

## Real type
Small-town Wisconsin commercial district of the late 19th and early 20th century: rows of one- and two-storey brick
and frame storefronts (two-part commercial blocks: a storefront below, offices or a hall above, a flat roof behind a
parapet with a cornice), frame false-front shops, a stone neoclassical bank, and dwellings behind. Main St runs north
to south (US 51 on the 1959 quad) and North St runs east to west (WI 73).

The second source for the overall layout is the 1959 USGS Wautoma quad. It shows the same junction of US 51 (Main) and
WI 73 (North), with the dense block of buildings south and east of it, and the store point traces to 2.6 m from Main
St (ASSETS.md).

## Proportions
- A two-storey brick front is about 1:1, frontage to height (7.3 m wide, 8.0 m to the roof, 8.9 m to the parapet).
- Main St width to building height is about 3.8:1, so the street reads very wide.
- Hardware store frontage to depth is 0.84: a near-square two-storey block.

## Parts list
- Front: storefront glass in two panes, a recessed centre door, a transom band, a blank sign band (no text), upper
  windows (2 to 6), and a parapet with a cornice.
- Side: a few windows.
- Back: the hardware store's rear door, where the truck stood (moving part), and the roof parapet.
- Moving parts: `hardware_store_door` and `hardware_store_rear_door`.

## Notes
The 1908 hardware lot is the fourth lot south of North St on the east side. 110 S Main fits that position, but the
1957 building and its use are not documented (fact file, section 6), so the placement is inferred. Names written on
the plans (Stark, Walker, lot uses) appear only as node names, never as signage.
