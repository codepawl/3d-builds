# Caption for posting a film

Two lines, plain enough for someone outside tech:

```
<Who> built the anatomy of <the machine>

<what it was, or what it does, in one everyday sentence>
```

Example second line: "The machine Germany used to hide its messages in World War II."

- No mechanism jargon (plugboard, reflector, armature) in the post caption; the figure explains that.
- Only facts from `facts.json` marked `verified`. Read the build's inferred and simplified items and keep
  them out.
- No "first", "only" or "invented" when someone else did it earlier; the lint rule applies to the post too.
- Credit the original Anatomy skill (github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy) where the post links the method.
- Never post a version the person who asked has not approved, and never post on their behalf without
  their explicit go for that exact video and text.

## Things rejected before (do not repeat)

- A dark theme by default; a plain block model with little real detail; a current path drawn as lines
  outside the parts.
- A permanent inset with a source rectangle on the model; opening on a still machine; taking it apart
  before the viewer has seen it work.
- Beeps, a constant bed, a limiter, anything loud, shrill or toy-like.
- A title bar, a footer wordmark, a caption that explains the mechanism.
