# Log flume (flume.glb)

**Real type:** Log flume water ride: open trough on steel trestles, conveyor lift, final drop into a splash pool, hollow-log boats seating 4-5.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final drop | about 15-23 m (75 ft on a large flume) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log_flume_(attraction) |
| Boat | 4.6 m long, 1.4 m wide, 4-5 riders in line | log_flume/03 (public domain) photo scale |
| Trough | 1.6 m inner width, 0.8 m deep | log_flume/02 photo scale against riders |
| Trestle height | 2-6 m on the run, lift to 16 m | log_flume/01 and /02 photo scale |

## Proportions

Boat length to width 3.3:1; drop slope 40-45 deg; lift slope about 30 deg.

## Parts

Trough, trestles, lift conveyor, drop, splash pool, station deck, log boats with seats. Moving: each boat along flume_path.json.
