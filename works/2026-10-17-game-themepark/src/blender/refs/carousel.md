# Carousel (rides.glb: carousel)

**Real type:** Two-row (abreast) carousel with galloping horses on brass poles, crown-topped tent canopy, rounding boards with lamps, centre column.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform diameter | 15 m | carousel/02 photo scale against riders; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carousel |
| Eave height / crown | 4.8 m / 8.5 m | carousel/02 photo scale |
| Horse | 1.6 m nose to tail, saddle 1.3 m above the platform | carousel/03 photo scale against the adult-sized horse |
| Rows | 2 rows x 12 horses, 2 chariots | carousel/02 |

## Proportions

Canopy diameter to eave height about 3.1:1; centre column diameter 0.17 of the platform.

## Parts

Base ring and steps, fence with gates, platform, centre column with panels, sweeps, poles, rounding boards with bulbs, canopy, crown and finial, horses, chariots. Moving: carousel_rotor (platform + canopy, about y) and each horse (bobs on its pole).
