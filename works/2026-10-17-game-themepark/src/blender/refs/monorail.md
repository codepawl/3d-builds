# Monorail (transport.glb: monorail_*)

**Real type:** Straddle-beam (ALWEG type) monorail: rubber-tyred cars straddling a concrete beam on T-pylons.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beam width | 0.6-0.9 m (2-3 ft); here 0.8 m, depth 1.5 m | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monorail |
| Car | 10 m long, 2.9 m wide, 3.3 m above the beam top | monorail/01 photo scale against the beam; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ALWEG |
| Pylon height | 6.5 m to beam top | monorail/01 photo scale |

## Proportions

Car length to height 3:1; skirt hides the beam to half its depth.

## Parts

Lead car with rounded nose and windscreen, middle cars, doors (separate), skirts, window band, beam segment, pylon with T-head. Moving: cars, doors.
