# Parade floats (transport.glb: float_*)

**Real type:** Parade float: a decorated flatbed trailer or self-propelled platform with skirts hiding the wheels and a raised centrepiece.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | 8 x 3 m, deck 1.0 m | parade_float/02, /03 photo scale; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parade_float |
| Centrepiece | up to 5.5 m tall | parade_float/01 photo scale |

## Proportions

Skirt to ground clearance 0.1 m; centrepiece 0.6 of the deck length.

## Parts

Deck, skirt with scallops, wheels, centrepiece (our own designs: cake, rocket, flower carriage), lamps.
