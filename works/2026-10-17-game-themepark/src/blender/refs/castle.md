# Fairytale castle (buildings.glb: castle)

**Real type:** Romanesque Revival castle type: a tall palas with steep roofs, round towers with conical turret caps, gatehouse arch.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall height | 34 m to the tallest spire (park scale; real type 65 m) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuschwanstein_Castle (150 m long complex); castle/01 photo scale |
| Footprint | 36 x 26 m | made-up park scale, proportions from castle/01, /02 |
| Turret diameter | 4-6 m with cones 1.6x their diameter | castle/01, /03 photo scale |

## Proportions

Keep height to width about 1.1:1; turret cone height to diameter 1.6:1.

## Parts

Keep, 4 corner towers, main spire, cones, crenellations, windows, gate arch, bridge, flags.
