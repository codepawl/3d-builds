# Coaster track, supports, lift, station (coaster.glb)

**Real type:** Steel coaster track with two tubular running rails over a box spine joined by ties, tubular columns on concrete footings, chain lift hill with service walkway.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lift hill height | 32.6 m crest, ~41 deg climb | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lift_hill (chain lift on the steepest climb); coaster_track/01 and /02 photo scale |
| First drop | about 30 m at up to 60 deg | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_coaster_elements |
| Rail tube diameter | 0.16 m; spine 0.48 x 0.4 m | photo scale coaster_track/03 against the 1.1 m gauge |
| Support columns | 0.45-0.85 m diameter, twin splayed legs above 14 m | coaster_track/01, /02, /03 photo scale |
| Station | 26 m x 9.6 m canopy, platform 0.95 m above grade | photo scale coaster_train/01, 5 cars + approach |

## Proportions

Layout 120 x 45 m footprint; lift run to rise about 1.15:1; ties every 2 m.

## Parts

Rails x2, spine, ties, columns, saddles, footings, cross braces, lift chain trough, walkway and handrail, drive house, brake fins, station deck, canopy, air gates, operator booth, status lamp, exit stairs.
