# Ferris wheel (rides.glb: ferris_wheel)

**Real type:** Fixed park Ferris wheel of the Wonder Wheel class: 24 enclosed cars on a twin-rim wheel carried by A-frame towers.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overall height | 46 m (150 ft) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Wheel |
| Passenger cars | 24 fully enclosed cars | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wonder_Wheel |
| Wheel diameter | 42 m, hub at 24 m | ferris_wheel/02 photo scale against the 46 m height |
| Gondola | 2.0 m wide x 2.4 m tall, 6 seats | ferris_wheel/03 photo scale; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferris_wheel |
| Rim spacing (twin rims) | 2.6 m | ferris_wheel/02 photo scale |

## Proportions

Height to diameter 1.1:1; tower legs splay to about 0.45 of the diameter at the base.

## Parts

Base platform, boarding deck with stairs and booth, 2 A-frame towers with braces, axle and hub, twin rims, 24 spokes per rim, rim cross ties, rim lamp ring, 24 gondolas with hangers. Moving: ferris_wheel_rotor (about x at the hub) and 24 gondolas (pivot at hanger, counter-rotate).
