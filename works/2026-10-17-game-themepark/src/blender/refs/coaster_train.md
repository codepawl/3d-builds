# Coaster train (coaster.glb: coaster_train_a / coaster_train_b)

**Real type:** Steel sit-down roller coaster train, 5 cars x 2 rows x 2 riders, polyurethane wheel assemblies (road, side guide, upstop) on tubular running rails.

## Dimensions

| Part | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car length (2 rows) | 2.6 m, pitch 2.9 m | photo scale coaster_train/01 and /02: rider rows about 1.0-1.1 m apart, two rows per car |
| Car width | 1.7 m | photo scale coaster_train/01: two seated adults side by side plus walls |
| Track gauge (rail centres) | 1.1 m | photo scale coaster_track/03 and coaster_train/03; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_coaster_elements |
| Wheel assembly | road wheel ~0.22 m dia above rail, upstop below, guide wheel at the side | coaster_train/03 (public domain close-up); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_coaster_train |
| Train length | 5 x 2.9 m = 14.5 m | derived |

## Proportions

Car length to width 1.5:1; seat back top 1.45 m above rail centre; lead car carries a nose fairing about 1 m long.

## Parts

Lead nose with lamps; per car: tub walls, floor, 4 bucket seats with headrests, 4 lap bars, side stripe and number plate, skirt, chassis, 4 wheel assemblies (road, upstop, guide, hub), rear bumper. Moving: each car (follows the spline).
