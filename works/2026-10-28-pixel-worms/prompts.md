# Prompts

The prompts are listed in order. The owner wrote them in Vietnamese, and they are translated here.

1. Make a pixel version of @Yokohara_h's worm swarm: worms follow the mouse, eat the creatures of a world, grow into a swarm and take the world over.
2. "The world is an open world, third-person view, not top-down."
3. "It is hard to look at, and it does not feel as fluid as the video on X."
4. "The worms are scattered, and after eating one animal they multiply far too much. An eaten animal should break into meat chunks, ribs and bones for them to eat."
5. "Fix the rest: softer rearing, the swarm sweeps into the woods, a big swarm in the last shot, redder worms."
6. "There are still too many worms at the start. Start with one and grow gradually. Add predators and traps that hurt the worms."
7. "The worms wiggle too much and it is unnatural. Move slowly and calmly, the body flowing, sliding like a smooth-skinned creature, like a snake."
8. "Besides crawling, they should really interact with objects: when they meet one, slowly climb over it."
9. "Turn this into a live web game for people to try. Credit the assets. Open source and free."
