# ed_building

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Emergency department front: ambulance bay under a drive-through canopy, separate walk-in entrance with sliding doors and a canopy, illuminated EMERGENCY-style sign plate (text-free in the model).

## Dimensions
- Ambulance bay canopy clearance 4.0-4.3 m (vehicle 2.9 m plus light bar) [S]; [P] ed_building/01 canopy about 1.5 x ambulance height
- Sliding door 2.4-2.8 m clear opening, 2.4 m high [S]; floor-to-floor 4.2 m [S]

## Proportions
canopy depth 8-10 m, about 1.3 ambulance lengths; walk-in canopy lighter and curved ([P] ed_building/02).

## Parts
wall modules with windows and panel joints, sliding doors (MOVE), canopy with columns, lights (glow), bollards, sign plate (glow), helipad, lamp posts, trees, kerbs.
