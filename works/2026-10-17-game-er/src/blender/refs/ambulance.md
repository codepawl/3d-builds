# ambulance

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
US Type III ambulance: cutaway van chassis-cab (E-450 class) with a square aluminium patient module; variant Type II high-roof van ambulance. Own livery: white, brand blue band, orange reflective stripe.

## Dimensions
- Overall length 6.7-7.0 m (Type III, 14 ft module) [S] and [P] ambulance/01: length = 7.6 wheel diameters of 0.82 m = 6.2 m plus bumpers
- Module body width 2.44 m (96 in, the KKK-A-1822F maximum body width) [F]
- Overall height 2.8-2.9 m to light bar [S]; [P] ambulance/01 module top at 3.4 wheel diameters = 2.8 m
- Wheelbase 4.0 m (158-176 in cutaway chassis) [S]; rear dual wheels; tyre diameter 0.80 m (LT225/75R16) [S]
- Patient compartment interior length 2.95 m, headroom 1.73 m (KKK-A-1822F minimums 116 in and 68 in) [F]
- Type II van: 6.0 m long, 2.0 m wide, 2.7 m high, wheelbase 3.66 m [S]

## Proportions
module length : cab length about 2.3 : 1; module top 0.45 m above cab roof; module floor 0.85 m above ground; rear doors split 50/50, each 0.65 m wide, 1.6 m high; side door forward on the kerb side.

## Parts
front: grille, headlamps, bumper, windscreen, warning lamps in grille, mirrors | side: cab door, cab window, fuel flap, curb-side entry door with window, two compartment doors, reflective stripe, wheels with hubs (single front, dual rear), steps | top: light bar on module front edge, roof AC unit, vents | back: two rear doors with windows (MOVE, hinge outer edge), step bumper, corner warning lamps, tail lamps. Moving: wheels, door_rear_L, door_rear_R, door_side; lights emissive.
