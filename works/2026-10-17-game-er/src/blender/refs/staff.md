# staff

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
ED staff in scrubs (nurse, doctor), porter in polo and trousers, paramedic in uniform with reflective bands.

## Dimensions
- Figure 1.75 m (adult average) used as scale everywhere [S]

## Proportions
scrub top V-neck with chest pocket, short sleeves; trousers loose; badge on lanyard; stethoscope around neck for doctors and nurses ([P] staff/01, staff/02).

## Parts
head, hair or scrub cap, torso, arms, hands, legs, shoes (clogs/trainers), badge, stethoscope, tablet. Armature bones and clips idle, walk, push_bed, chart, sit.
