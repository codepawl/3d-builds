# nurses_station

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
ED nurses' station and triage desk: low counter with a raised transaction ledge, workstations, status board; triage desk is a smaller two-sided booth by the walk-in entrance.

## Dimensions
- Counter height 0.75 m work surface, 1.05-1.1 m ledge, depth 0.6 m [S] (ADA desk height 0.71-0.86 m)
- [P] nurses_station/01: counter about 0.6 of standing staff height; monitors 0.45 m wide

## Proportions
U or L shaped counter 4-6 m long; ledge band 0.3 m above the work surface.

## Parts
counter body with panels, ledge, work surface, 3-4 monitors (screens emissive), keyboards, chairs, tracking board on a post (emissive), sign plate, phone, chart rack.
