# lab_analyzer

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Clinical chemistry analyser (floor-standing) plus centrifuge on a lab bench.

## Dimensions
- Analyser about 1.5 m long, 0.8 m deep, 1.2 m high [S]; [P] lab_analyzer/02 sample carousel 0.5 m diameter

## Proportions
low cabinet with a glass lid over the carousel; touch screen on an arm at one end.

## Parts
cabinet with panels and vents, glass lid, sample carousel and reagent rotor, probe arm, touch screen (emissive), status lamp, tube racks; bench with centrifuge and microscope.
