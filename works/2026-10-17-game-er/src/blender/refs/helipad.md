# helipad

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Hospital ground or rooftop heliport: square touchdown and lift-off area (TLOF) inside a final approach area (FATO), hospital marking, perimeter lights, wind cone.

## Dimensions
- TLOF at least the design helicopter's rotor diameter, 12 m for a 10.2 m rotor; FATO 1.5 x overall length = 18 m [F] FAA AC 150/5390-2D
- Perimeter lights every 7.5 m or less, green, flush or 0.1 m high [F]
- Wind cone 2.4 m long on a 3-4 m mast [F]
- [P] helipad/01 rooftop pad on a hospital wing roughly 3 floors (12 m) above the street

## Proportions
pad square 18 m with a 12 m TLOF inset; marking cross and H about 0.6 of the TLOF.

## Parts
deck slab, TLOF outline, red cross/H marking (own design, not a sign of a real hospital), green perimeter lights (glow), floodlights, wind cone on mast, safety net/railing on the edge.
