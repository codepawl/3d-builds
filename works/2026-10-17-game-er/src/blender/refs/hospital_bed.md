# hospital_bed

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Electric acute-care hospital bed: four-section deck, head and foot boards, split side rails, base on castors.

## Dimensions
- Overall 2.2 m long, 1.0 m wide with rails, deck height 0.4-0.8 m, castors 125-150 mm [S]
- [P] hospital_bed/02: length about 2.2 x width; head board 0.95 m high from floor; rails 0.35 m above mattress

## Proportions
head board taller than foot board (1.0 : 0.8); rails two per side, head rails 0.7 m long.

## Parts
base frame with cover, 4 castors (MOVE), lift columns, deck with mattress, backrest section (MOVE), knee break, head board, foot board with control panel, split side rails, bumper wheels, IV socket.
