# ct_scanner

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Multislice CT scanner: ring gantry with 70-80 cm bore on a pedestal, patient couch with sliding table top.

## Dimensions
- Gantry 2.0-2.2 m wide, 1.9-2.0 m high, 0.9-1.0 m deep; bore 0.70-0.80 m [S]
- Couch 2.4 m long base, table top travel 1.6-2.0 m, height 0.5-1.0 m [S]
- [P] ct_scanner/03 opened gantry: rotor ring about 1.6 m against the person beside it

## Proportions
bore centre at 0.53 of gantry height; gantry face rounded square with a tilted outer cover; couch base 0.45 m wide, table top 0.5 m wide.

## Parts
gantry cover, bore ring with laser markers, front control panels with small screens, status lights (glow), pedestal, couch base with bellows, sliding table top (MOVE along forward), head rest, control console behind lead glass with two monitors.
