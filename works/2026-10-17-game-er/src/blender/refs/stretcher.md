# stretcher

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Ambulance powered cot (Ferno/Stryker class) and ED transport stretcher (trolley).

## Dimensions
- Cot length 2.06 m, width 0.58 m, height 0.36-1.05 m [S]; [P] stretcher/01 cot width about 0.55 of the 1.1 m door opening
- ED stretcher 2.1 m long, 0.8 m wide (with rails), deck height 0.55-0.9 m, 150-200 mm castors [S]

## Proportions
mattress 0.1 m thick; backrest 0.7 m of deck length, raised up to 60 degrees; X-frame legs on the cot.

## Parts
mattress with orange/blue cover, backrest (MOVE, hinge at 0.7 m from foot), side rails, IV pole socket, push handles, X-frame legs (cot), base with bumper and 4 castors (MOVE), loading wheels at the head.
