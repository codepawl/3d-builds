# helicopter

Reference images are for modelling only; never shown in the app or film.

## Real type
Light twin-engine medical helicopter, EC135/H135 class: pod-and-boom fuselage, 4-blade bearingless main rotor, ducted fan (fenestron) tail, skids, rear clamshell doors for the stretcher. Own scheme.

## Dimensions
- Fuselage length 10.2 m, height 3.51 m [W] EC135 wikitext specifications
- Main rotor diameter 10.2 m; length with rotors turning 12.16 m [W]
- Skid track 2.0 m, cabin width 1.5 m [P] helicopter/01 (skid track about 0.57 of cabin length)
- Max take-off weight 2,910 kg [W] (used for the app's sheet, not the model)

## Proportions
cabin pod 4.0 m of the 10.2 m fuselage; tail boom tapers to a fenestron of 1.0 m diameter with a vertical fin above; rotor mast at 2.9 m; horizontal stabiliser with end plates at 70% of length.

## Parts
front: rounded nose, large windscreen, chin windows | side: pilot door, sliding cabin door, engine cowlings with exhausts, tail boom, stabiliser, fin, fenestron | top: 4-blade rotor head (MOVE about up), engine intakes | back: clamshell doors (MOVE), skid gear with cross tubes. Moving: rotor_main, rotor_tail, door_clam_L/R; beacons and nav lights emissive.
