# Miter gate leaf, rolling gate, valve house, signal post

Written from memory, numbers marked **verify**; no images downloaded.

**Real type:** steel miter gate leaves (two leaves meeting in a V that points upstream) of the original Panama locks, hollow buoyant girder construction; a rolling gate (a box gate that slides sideways into a recess) as on the newer locks.

## Dimensions
| Item | Value | Sources (verify) |
|---|---|---|
| Leaf length | 19.2 m modelled (real about 19.8 m, 65 ft) | PCA gate fact sheet; Wikipedia "Panama Canal locks" |
| Leaf thickness | 3.0 m (real about 2 m, 7 ft) | PCA |
| Leaf height | 20, 23, 25.5 m (real 14 to 25 m depending on the chamber) | PCA |
| Miter angle | 17 to 22 degrees off the cross-chamber line | USACE EM 1110-2-2703 lock gates; general lock design |
| Rolling gate | 35 x 7 x 24 m modelled; the newer gates are about 57 m long, 10 m thick, 33 m tall | PCA expansion fact sheet |
| Valve house | 3.4 x 3.0 x 4 m, stem rises 1.2 m when open | design choice |

## Proportions
Leaf length : height = 19.2 : 23 = 0.83; skin plate on one face, 5 horizontal girders and 5 vertical frames on the other.

## Parts list
Skin plate, girders (web + outer flange), vertical frames, round quoin post at the hinge, miter post with rubber seal at the toe, grating walkway on top with a yellow handrail, hinge pintle bracket, sill shoe. Rolling gate: box body, vertical and horizontal stiffeners, top walkway with rail, trolley wheels. Valve house: base, roof, door, stem, hand wheel, open and closed lamps. Signal post: pole, plinth, head with 3 (a) or 2 (b) lamp objects.
Moving: gate leaf rotates about the hinge (its origin); valve stem translates up; valve wheel turns about its axis; lamps swap materials.
