# Lock chamber (walls, centre wall, floor, sill, mule track)

All numbers are written from memory and marked **verify**; no reference images were downloaded (the user has not approved downloads). The compare sheets use a dimension blockout drawn from this sheet.

**Real type:** gravity-filled, three-step flight of twin lock chambers with miter gates, like the original Panama Canal locks (opened 1914). Made-up canal, made-up names.

| Mule track | rack rail between two running rails, gauge 1.5 m modelled, track centre 2.5 m from the inner edge | PCA locomotive fact sheet |

## Proportions
- Chamber length : width = 305 : 33.5 = 9.1 : 1.
- Wall height : chamber width = 25 : 33.5 = 0.75.
- Module length 30.5 m = one tenth of a chamber.

## Parts list (every visible part)
Side wall with stepped outer face and vertical inner face, kerb strip, worked top slab, outer curb, expansion and construction joints, ladder and depth-gauge variants, lamp posts, bollards, manholes; gate-pocket wall; centre wall with two vertical inner faces and a central service strip, plinths for valve houses; chamber floor with 40 culvert ports; sill block with the miter V ridge and hinge pintle blocks; mule track with sleepers, running rails, rack with teeth, cable pit covers.
Moving parts: none (gates, valves and mules are separate families).
