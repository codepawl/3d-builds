# Mule (rack-and-pinion electric towing locomotive)

Written from memory, numbers marked **verify**; no images downloaded.

**Real type:** the Panama Canal "mulas": small powerful electric locomotives that run on a rack track on the lock wall top and pull ships with steel cables from a vertical winch. This design is made up.

## Dimensions
| Item | Value | Sources (verify) |
|---|---|---|
| Length over buffers | 10.4 m modelled (real about 10 to 11 m) | PCA locomotive fact sheet; Wikipedia "Panama Canal locks" |
| Width | 3.3 m (real about 3 m) | same |
| Height to the roof | 5.0 m, beacon to 5.3 m | design choice from remembered photos |
| Weight | about 55 t real | PCA |
| Wheels | 1.1 m diameter, 8 in two bogies | design choice |
| Track | gauge 1.5 m, rack in the centre | verify |

## Proportions
Length : width = 3.2 : 1; hood about 3.8 m long, cab 4.2 m, machinery housing 2.9 m; cab is about 1.8 times as tall as the hood.

## Parts list
Chassis, front hood with grille, cab with windscreen and side windows, rear machinery housing with resistor louvres, roof overhang and fan, handrails, buffers, 8 flanged wheels (separate nodes), rack pinion, capstan drum on the hood, cable arm with fairlead sheave, two headlamps, beacon.
Moving: wheels (spin about the axle), capstan (about its vertical axis), cable arm (swings about its hinge). Lamps swap materials.
