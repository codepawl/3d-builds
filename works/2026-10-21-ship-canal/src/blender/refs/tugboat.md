## Control houses, control center, light tower, buoys
Real types: a lock control house on the centre wall (a glazed booth that sees both lanes), a canal operations control center (a low hall with a control tower and a wall display), a floodlight pole, and IALA lateral channel buoys.
Sizes (design choices, verify against the PCA administration building and IALA buoy tables): control house 10.4 x 8.4 m, 5 m (a) or 9 m (b) tall; control center 66 x 32 m podium, hall 14 m, tower 26 m plus glass cab and mast; light tower 18 m with 4 lamp heads; buoy about 4 m tall, 2 m diameter.
