# RMS Titanic, an illustrated cutaway plate in 3D

Look: Illustrated cutaway plate (`references/looks.md` of the visualize-in-3d skill). Reference image:
an illustrated encyclopedia cutaway plate of a medieval castle (reference picture not included). Shape: a live three.js page the viewer can
orbit and read, the encyclopedia plate brought to life. The first try is for An to look at; nothing is posted.

## What people must see

The Titanic from her starboard side, three-quarter high view, with the camera-side hull cut away from bow to stern
so every deck reads as a stacked stage: first class luxury at the top, third class lower, and the fire and coal of
the boiler rooms at the bottom. The contrast between the decks is the story.

## The real ship (numbers to check against sources and mark measured/inferred in `facts.md`)

- Length 269.1 m (882 ft 9 in), beam 28.2 m, keel to funnel tops about 53 m, draught about 10.5 m.
- Four funnels, buff with black tops; the fourth was a dummy for ventilation. Two masts. Black hull, white
  superstructure, red antifouling below the waterline, a thin gold line along the sheer.
- Decks from the top: Boat deck, A (promenade), B, C, D (saloon), E, F, G, Orlop, Tank top.
- Rooms to build and label (place them where they really were):
  - Boat deck: bridge and wheelhouse, Marconi wireless room, gymnasium, 20 lifeboats (14 standard, 2 cutters, 4
    collapsibles), the forward Grand Staircase dome.
  - A deck: first class promenade, lounge, reading and writing room, smoking room.
  - Forward Grand Staircase from Boat deck down to E deck.
  - D deck: first class dining saloon and reception room.
  - F deck: swimming pool and Turkish bath; third class dining saloons.
  - G deck and Orlop: squash court, mail room, cargo holds, third class berths forward and aft.
  - Six boiler rooms with 29 boilers and 159 furnaces, coal bunkers, stokers and trimmers.
  - Engine room: two four-cylinder triple expansion engines, one low pressure turbine, three shafts and propellers.
  - 15 watertight bulkheads making 16 compartments; the crow's nest on the foremast.
- About 2,200 people aboard on the maiden voyage. Every room gets figures doing its job.

## Page

- Title "RMS TITANIC" in engraved small caps, intro: built in Belfast by Harland and Wolff, maiden voyage April 1912,
  the largest moving object ever made at the time (check each fact).
- Small inset of the whole ship from outside, top left.
- 10 to 14 callouts (bold heading, 2 to 4 lines) in the paper margins with leader lines.
- Bottom row "People aboard": Captain, officer, Marconi operator, first class passenger, steward, third class
  passenger, stoker, lookout. Each a full figure, a bold name and a two-line role.
- The sea is a loose teal watercolour wash at the waterline that fades into the paper; no hard water plane edge.

## Interaction

Orbit within limits (keep the cut side facing the viewer), zoom, hover or tap a callout to fly to its room.
Works at phone width: callouts collapse to numbered pins and a card.

## Views
